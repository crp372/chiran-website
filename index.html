About

I’m a fourth-year medical student at Humanitas University in Milan, Italy, exploring research and clinical interests in cardiology and orthopedic surgery.

My path to medicine runs circuitously through literature. I earned a BA in Literature and Creative Writing from NYU Abu Dhabi, then completed an MSt in World Literatures in English at the University of Oxford as an Ertegun Scholar in 2020.

At Humanitas, I teach a course on AI and the humanities in the Master’s program in Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence in Health Sciences. I also teach presentation skills as part of the Scientific English course for students in the Physiotherapy, Biomedical Laboratory Techniques, and Medical Radiology Techniques programs. I previously taught English A: Language and Literature in the IB Diploma Programme at Ullens School in Nepal. I’m presently building tools aimed at making medical education interactive and accessible.
