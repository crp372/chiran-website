Privacy

This notice covers chiranpandey.com and coronary.chiranpandey.com. In short: no cookies, no advertising, no tracking across other sites, and I never see who you are.

Who is responsible

Chiran Pandey, Milan, Italy, is the data controller for these sites. You can reach me at chiranrpandey@gmail.com.

Hosting

Both sites are hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the sites from abuse, Cloudflare necessarily processes technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. Cloudflare acts as my processor. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Visitor statistics

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies or store anything on your device, and it does not follow you to other websites. It shows me aggregate figures only: which pages are viewed, the referring site, general device and browser type, country, and page loading speed. I cannot identify individual visitors from it. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in understanding how the sites are used.

Content loaded from other services

The coronary view simulator loads fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from cdnjs and jsDelivr. When your browser fetches these files, those providers receive your IP address and browser details, as with any web request. The main site, chiranpandey.com, loads nothing from third parties apart from the statistics script above.

Cookies

These sites set no cookies.

Email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply, and I don't share them.

International transfers

Cloudflare and Google may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States. Both are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data about you, restrict or object to its processing, and complain to a supervisory authority. In Italy that is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. Since I don't hold data that identifies visitors, most requests will concern emails you've sent me. Write to the address above.
